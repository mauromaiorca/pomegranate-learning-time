Pomegranate Learning Time – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 (v1.14)

Pomegranate Learning Time is a study and break timer developed by Mauro Maiorca, IIBIOMI (IIBIOMI.it).

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store remotely, share or sell any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs, and it does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your timing settings and, if you use named sessions, the names you enter (for example an educator's pupils, or your own name), each person's timing, the ratings given to study blocks, optional notes, photos of the work and the session history are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere by the app. Deleting a name in the Summary screen, or uninstalling the app, deletes this data.

Photos of the work are taken with your phone's own camera app or chosen from your gallery, only when you ask for them. The app keeps a reduced copy in its private storage, attached to the session, and does not need camera or storage permissions. You can delete any photo from the Summary screen.

The Summary screen can export a PDF report, a JSON file of sessions or a backup file, and can import sessions from such a file. These files are created only when you press the button, and saved where you choose (for example Downloads or Google Drive) or shared through the app you choose. Nothing is sent automatically. Restore reads a backup file that you select.

The app also stores a few automatic safety copies of your data in its private storage, and Android may include the app's data in your device backup, according to your phone's backup settings. Photos are excluded from the cloud backup.

If you record data about other people (for example pupils), you are responsible for processing it lawfully, under the GDPR and any rules of your organisation.

Permissions

  • Alarms and reminders, notifications, full-screen notifications and the alarm service: used only to ring at the end of each study block and break, like an alarm clock, even with the app closed or the screen off.
  • Run at startup: used only to reschedule a running timer after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration and wake lock: used only while the alarm rings.

The app has no internet, camera, location, contacts or storage permission.
