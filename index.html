Pomegranate Learning Time – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 (v1.14.1)

Pomegranate Learning Time is a study and break timer with a logbook for organising study time, developed by Mauro Maiorca, IIBIOMI (IIBIOMI.it).

Who the app is for

Adults who supervise study sessions, such as parents, tutors and educators, and students aged 16 or over. The app is not directed at children under 16. An adult may record a learner's name and ratings in the app; that record stays under the adult's control and does not reach the developer.

Data we collect

None through the app. The app sends nothing to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs, and it does not use the internet.

If you e-mail the developer or join the optional WhatsApp group, what you write is handled outside the app. Please do not include pupils' names, photos, reports or health details.

Data stored on your device

Your timing settings and, if you use named sessions, the names you enter (for example an educator's pupils, or your own name), each person's timing, the ratings given to study blocks, optional notes, photos of the work and the session history are saved on your device, in the app's private storage. The app itself never sends them anywhere.

Deleting a name in the Summary screen removes it and its photos from the app at once. The automatic safety copies described below can keep the name until later saves replace them. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this from the device.

Photos of the work are taken with your phone's own camera app or chosen from your gallery, only when you ask for them. The app keeps a reduced copy in its private storage, attached to the session, and does not need camera or storage permissions. You can delete any photo from the Summary screen.

The Summary screen can export a PDF report, a JSON file of sessions or a backup file, and can import sessions from such a file. These files are created only when you press the button, and saved where you choose (for example Downloads or Google Drive) or shared through the app you choose. Nothing is sent automatically. Restore reads a backup file that you select.

The app also stores a few automatic safety copies of your data in its private storage.

Android may copy the app's data to your own Google backup according to your phone's settings. Photos are excluded from this cloud copy. On Android 12 and later, the app asks Android to skip the cloud copy when the phone cannot encrypt it on the device, for example with no screen lock. When you move to a new phone, Android's device-to-device transfer can also copy the app's data, photos included. A copy in your Google backup is kept and removed under Google's rules, not by the app.

If you record data about other people (for example pupils), you are responsible for processing it lawfully, under the GDPR and any rules of your organisation.

Professional use

Whoever uses the app for pupils professionally is the data controller, needs their own legal basis and notice to families, and should not enter diagnoses, medication or therapy details. On a personal phone, Android's backup can place pupils' names in your personal Google account, so check this against your organisation's rules.

Permissions

  • Alarms and reminders, notifications, full-screen notifications and the alarm service: used only to ring at the end of each study block and break, like an alarm clock, even with the app closed or the screen off.
  • Run at startup: used only to reschedule a running timer after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration and wake lock: used only while the alarm rings.

The app has no internet, camera, location, contacts or storage permission.
