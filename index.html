Pomegranate Learning Time – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Pomegranate Learning Time is a study and break timer developed by Mauro Maiorca, IIBIOMI (IIBIOMI.it).

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store remotely, share or sell any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs, and it does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your timing settings and, if you use named sessions, the names you enter (for example an educator's pupils, or your own name), each person's timing, the seed scores given to study blocks, optional notes and the session history are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere by the app. Deleting a name in the Summary screen, or uninstalling the app, deletes this data.

The Summary screen has a Share button. It only shares a text summary when you press it, through the app you choose (for example e-mail); nothing is sent automatically.

Permissions

Vibration: used only to vibrate together with the alarm.

Children

The app is suitable for children. Because no data is collected, no personal information from children is collected either.
